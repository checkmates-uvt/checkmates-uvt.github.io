Sign up for our Chessnight event

What: Duo Chess (Hand-Brain) event w/ TSEA Link!
- Individual signup (FREE), teams are announced the day before
- Least experienced is paired with most experienced, second least with second most, etc
- The more experienced player names a piece, the other player plays a move with that piece (castling is a king move)
- No other game-related communication between teamed players is allowed during the game

When: Oct 1st 18:30 (open at 18:00 for setup and questions)
Where: On campus, exact location will follow
Who: Anyone that knows the rules of chess and is either a student or faculty

Format: (10min + 5s per move), count on 30 min a round
- 4-8 teams: Full round robin
- 9-12 teams: Swiss + t4 single elim bracket
- 13+ teams: Swiss + t8 single elim bracket

Prize: Will be announced/shown at a later time, we intend to obtain a trophy of sorts :D

Sign up here: https://forms.gle/5m2MadQwjNVfFVPM9

If you have any questions about this event or how it is managed, feel free to ask here or text the Event Coordinator (Lars).

Tournaments & events

Separate from weekly meetings, we also join and organize tournaments and special events. These range from rapid arenas and open bracket tournaments to lessons and more. Keep an eye on the agenda to find these events and join in!

Open chess

We hold casual chess meetings every friday at 14:45 in the Dante building. See the agenda for the next meeting. Feel free to drop by anytime during these hours for a game or to learn more about chess. All skill levels are welcome!
